Word Blast background-music pass: a playful cartoon puzzle-adventure
soundtrack, integrated into the existing AudioManager. All gameplay sound
effects are untouched (full effects suite re-verified — see §4).

## 1. The track

`public/assets/audio/music/word-blast-theme.wav` — **original
composition**, rendered procedurally by `scripts/generate-audio.mjs`
(`buildCartoonTheme`), same synthesis pipeline as every other game sound.
No external samples, nothing copied from any commercial game — no
licensing constraints.

- **Style**: light cartoon orchestral — bouncy pizzicato-string
  oom-pah (root/fifth bass + offbeat chord stabs), marimba lead melody,
  xylophone octave doubling, glockenspiel sparkles/fills, soft
  percussion (shaker eighths, woodblock backbeat, whispered brush,
  soft thud). Deliberately NOT jazz: straight eighths (no swing), folk
  I/IV/V/vi harmony (no 7th-chord comping), no saxophone/lounge leads,
  no heavy drums, no synths.
- **Form**: C major, 124 BPM, 4/4, 32 bars ≈ 61.9 s — A (marimba theme)
  → A' (xylophone double + backbeat) → B (glockenspiel/xylophone
  call-and-response bridge over Am–F–C–G) → A'' (full band). The final
  bar strikes the leading tone (B5) on the last eighth so it is still
  ringing when the loop wraps onto the opening E5 — the seam is a
  musical continuation, not an energy hole.
- **Seamless loop**: rendered with reverb-tail fold-back (loopify
  crossfade), RMS −19.5 dBFS, peak −5.5 dB. The generator QC enforces
  seam continuity (no sample step; wrapped-seam energy within 6 dB of
  the neighbouring groove) on every regeneration.

## 2. Playback engine (js/systems/AudioManager.js)

One dedicated single-voice engine (`_music`), mirroring the sling-voice
pattern. The theme NEVER goes through `play()` (`play()` refuses the
music key) so it can never hit cooldowns, voice caps or one-shot
teardown.

- **Start**: `startMusic()` — from the PLAY press only (MenuScene) or
  the music toggle. Never auto-starts on boot/attach/level entry; a
  saved `music:true` is intent-free. Idempotent: an existing voice is
  never restacked or restarted; rapid PLAY double-taps stay one voice.
  Fades in over 700 ms. If the asset is missing the start is dropped
  and counted (never crashes), and `want` keeps the intent for a later
  master re-enable.
- **Continuity**: manager-owned (game sound manager, not scene state),
  so it survives Menu → LevelSelect → GameScene → level restarts
  without ever restarting between questions (verified by position
  continuity across a real scene restart).
- **Pause/resume**: rides the existing `pauseAll/resumeAll` choke
  point — same voice, same position, no restart, no stacking.
- **Ducking**: `duckForSpeech(true/false)` (QuestionCard pronunciation)
  now also ramps the music to 45 % and back over ~280 ms, alongside the
  existing sfx ducking — the English pronunciation stays clear.
- **Level**: manifest 0.9 × music bus 0.55 × master 0.9 ≈ 0.45 — the
  bed sits ~17 dB under shot/impact transients (measured in-browser).
  `setBusVolume('music'|'master')` live-updates the playing voice.
- **ON/OFF**: `setMusicEnabled()` persists the choice and starts/stops
  the live voice (fade-out 450 ms). The existing pause-menu toggle and
  HUD state already wire into it. Master mute covers the music path
  (verified functional silence at the output tap); while master-muted
  the single instance keeps its loop position, so re-enabling continues
  seamlessly instead of restarting.
