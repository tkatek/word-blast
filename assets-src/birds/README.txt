Word Blast — 6 birds

Contains 6 transparent PNG sprites at original 1254x1254 resolution (game_ready/) and 4096x4096 upscaled versions (4K_upscaled/).

The 4K files are upscaled from the generated originals; they do not contain additional native fine detail.

Bird roles: red = basic, blue = split, yellow = speed, black = bomb, purple = ice, green = optional bonus character.
Use game_ready for performance-sensitive Phaser rendering.
